01 — About

Freelance appointment setting, done by hand

I work as an independent contractor for online businesses and coaching companies, handling the part of sales most founders don't have time for: reaching out to prospective clients, having a real conversation to confirm fit, and getting a qualified appointment on the calendar.

Every prospect I talk to is contacted directly, qualified against the client's stated criteria, and only booked once they meet it. No appointment goes on the calendar just to hit a number.

I've worked primarily with coaching businesses and early-stage startups — offers where the sale happens on a call, and the calendar is the pipeline.

02 — Process

Three steps, every call

01

Contact

I reach out to prospects from a provided list or inbound inquiry, working through it methodically and keeping a record of every attempt and outcome.

02

Qualify

Each conversation confirms budget, timeline, and fit against the client's criteria before anything gets scheduled — protecting the client's calendar from no-fit calls.

03

Book & nurture

Qualified prospects are booked directly onto the sales calendar. I follow up to confirm and reduce no-shows, so the appointment is still warm when it's time to talk.
